## Method

- **Independent reviewer:** a separate read-only Explore agent. It was given the evidence locations and defect classes A to G (coverage omissions, unknown costs as zero, false security claims, branch/account/remote leakage, skipped native tests, source changes after evidence, repeated unit runs counted as audits). It was not given the auditor's conclusions.
- **Auditor:** re-read every reviewer anchor. Nine findings were kept or extended, and two were rejected (see below). The auditor also re-observed the stale evidence at HEAD (next section).

They are not three audits, and repeated unit runs are not counted as audits. This record is the one independent audit.
- **Safety:** 0 live calls. No Grok, Claude or Codex binary was executed. 0 images.
