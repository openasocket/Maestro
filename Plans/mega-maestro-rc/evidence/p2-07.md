### Findings the checks record

- One facade, no second projection: the pane, the `enterprise:*` IPC
  handlers and the ten bridge messages read `EnterpriseService.managed`, so
  the standing `managed status` prints is the standing the pane shows, and
  the keys `managed locks` lists are the keys the Settings badge marks.
- An inbox act is attributed in the facade, not the handler:
  `transportSpawnActor(getActingTransport())` names the actor for a CLI, a
  Web Login user or a paired device, and a paired device with no registered
  actor is `denied unauthenticated` with nothing recorded.
- Managed mode is never an Encore flag: `ENCORE_FEATURE_DEFAULTS` gained no
  key, and a policy pins a flag through `encore.<flag>` (always a boolean,
  refused bare); the pin wins over the store on every sink and the lifecycle
  stops a pinned-off service the moment the policy lands.
- `inbox` stays an alias of the Notification Center; the managed inbox
  answers to `managed-inbox`, and the registry test pins both.
- No secret crosses a command line: the enrollment request's invitation and
  key-possession proof live in the file the CLI relays whole, and no `--json`
  output of any verb carries one.
